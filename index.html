MoSCoW Classification

Stage 1: Classify each issue. Only MUST HAVE and top SHOULD HAVE advance to RICE scoring.

Must Have
Revenue-critical, SLA breach, core function broken
Should Have
Important, workaround exists, affects >10% users
Could Have
Nice to have, low urgency, <5% impact
Won't Have
Noise, out of scope, duplicate, single low-value request
🏷️
No issues to classify

RICE Scoring

Stage 2: Score forwarded issues. RICE = (Reach × Impact × Confidence) ÷ Effort

Reach
% or # of accounts affected per sprint
1–10 scale
Impact
Effect on ROAS, conversion, retention
0.5 / 1 / 2 / 3
Confidence
Certainty in Reach + Impact estimates
20% / 50% / 80% / 100%
Effort
Eng + Design cost in person-weeks
0.5 / 1 / 2 / 4 / 8
